# Chapter 1 Surface Entrance — Art Vertical Slice

Status: **production benchmark for 0.0.9 World & Audio Cohesion RC**

The previous `echoes_surface_art_v2.png` is now classified as **prototype compatibility art**, not final/polished Chapter 1 art. Existing maps/saves may still reference it, but new Surface Entrance art must use the production packs described here.

## 1. Production packs

Generated by `tools/generate_surface_vertical_slice.py` into:

`core/src/main/assets/environment/echoes/ch1_surface/`

| Pack | Size | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `grass.png` | 128×32 | sparse grass, damp grass, trample, flowers, stones, bare patches |
| `forest.png` | 128×64 | forest edge A/B, deep canopy, bush, stump, fallen log, underbrush, mossy rocks |
| `road.png` | 128×32 | verges, ruts, stones, mud, trample, wheel scar, invading weeds |
| `river.png` | 128×32 | upper/lower banks, reeds, ripples, stones, foam, wet grass, roots |
| `bridge.png` | 64×48 | one 4×3 tile authored timber crossing |
| `camp_ruin.png` | 192×64 | one 6×4 camp scene and one 6×4 dungeon-mouth scene |

The generator is the editable source. It uses explicit RGBA pixel operations; no source art is stored as an embedded/base64 PNG blob.

## 2. Pixel/material grammar

- World grid remains SPD-native **16×16 px per map tile**.
- Production environment art must be placed and scaled at integer pixel ratios only.
- Transparent overlays are preferred for material variation; base terrain remains responsible for the continuous floor/water field.
- Do not create detail by distributing uniform random noise across every tile.
- Use clustered, authored variation tied to material or location: road wear belongs on road beats, damp grass belongs near water, trampling belongs around camp/road, deep shadows belong inside forest masses.
- Full black is reserved for deepest cavities/contact separation. Ordinary outlines use dark chromatic values.
- Highlight areas stay small. At this scale silhouette and value grouping take priority over micro-detail.

## 3. Six material rules

### Grass / weeds

Open ground must not read as one repeated noisy carpet. Use sparse variants and regional changes: normal clearing, damp creek edge, trampled camp, stone/flower accents. The base grass tile should remain visible between accents.

### Forest / edge

`WALL` may remain the collision rule, but it must not read as a flat green wall. Use at least a two-stage visual depth: readable edge trees/underbrush, then darker canopy/deep-forest mass. Canopies may live on the wall layer so they overlap like raised terrain.

### Dirt road / edges

The road must remain readable as the main route without becoming a uniform brown ribbon. Use verges, wheel ruts, stones, mud and grass intrusion at curated intervals. Road decoration must not be placed at identical cadence.

### River / banks

Water animation remains engine-owned. Production overlays supply the missing transition grammar: wet bank, reeds, stones, roots, foam/ripples. The creek should read as water occupying a natural channel, not a blue strip painted on grass.

### Wooden bridge

The Surface Entrance crossing is a single **4×3 tile authored structure**, not a 1×1 generic bridge icon. It must have bridgeheads, irregular plank widths, posts/rail elements, nails/gaps and a contact shadow while keeping the walkable center visually obvious.

### Camp / dungeon mouth

These are scene anchors, not icons. The camp uses a stepped tent silhouette, fire ring, crate/bedroll and trampled edge. The dungeon mouth uses irregular broken masonry, moss, a dark stepped opening and visible threshold/stair glints. Both render below actors so the hero/NPC silhouette is never hidden by a decorative scene image.

## 4. Character same-screen scale contract

Surface characters are evaluated against SPD human NPC scale, not against enlarged concept art.

- SPD Blacksmith reference frame: **13×16**.
- Farmer: **16×16** frame; four frames across the sheet.
- Bird: **16×16** frame; four frames across the sheet.
- Wolf: **16×16** frame; four frames across the sheet.
- Civilian set: **16×16** frame; eight two-frame silhouettes.
- Donkey + cart: intentional wide exception at **32×16** per frame; it is staged as a wide scene actor and is not allowed to fake north/south travel with a side-view sheet.

Acceptance questions for every character:

1. Does the foot/contact baseline sit naturally beside an SPD hero/NPC?
2. Is the head/body mass readable at 1× without zooming?
3. Does the darkest outline occupy a similar visual weight to SPD sprites?
4. Are highlights limited enough that the sprite does not look pasted from a brighter asset pack?
5. Is idle motion quieter than movement/attack motion?
6. For a wide actor, does the animation truthfully match the direction shown on screen?

CI validates the current frame-sheet dimensions so a later repaint cannot silently break this scale contract.

## 5. Filtering / scaling contract

Pixel art must use nearest-neighbor/no-filter scaling. No bilinear/bicubic resize is allowed for runtime pixel assets. A source pixel must remain an integer block on screen. The generator itself performs no resampling.

## 6. Surface Entrance acceptance gate

The deterministic `artifacts/ch1_surface_vertical_slice_preview.png` is a **source-art review board only**. It is useful for checking silhouettes, palette and major material relationships, but it is **not an in-engine acceptance screenshot**.

0.0.9 may call Surface Entrance art “polished” only after an actual gameplay capture passes all of the following:

- No large area reads as repeated grass wallpaper.
- Forest boundary has edge/depth and does not read as a green wall.
- Road remains readable but no longer looks like a uniform brown carpet.
- Creek has visible bank transitions and a clearly authored bridge.
- Camp and dungeon mouth read as scene anchors at normal gameplay zoom.
- Farmer, donkey, bird and hero remain readable without any asset-pack mismatch.
- No overlay hides a transition, NPC, item or walkable route.
- A first-time player can identify the road north without relying on the minimap.

Only after this gameplay screenshot passes should the production material language be propagated to Old King's Road or Morningcreek Outskirts.
